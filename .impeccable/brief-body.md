# iPhone core surfaces
Mode: Operate. Invited Chinese/English users browse their private original media, transfer it safely and control their account. Use native SwiftUI, iOS 17+, with Library, Transfers and Account tabs. Testing uses synthetic assets in isolated AWS.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A quiet, chronological native photo library; originals lead, while upload and erasure state remain explicit.
OWN-WORLD: Semantic iOS surfaces, San Francisco text styles, one system-blue interaction tint, the existing cloud/puzzle brand mark, native navigation stacks and grouped account forms.
STORY: Sign in, acknowledge policies, browse by capture date, open an original, save or upload it, inspect transfers and storage, and request server-side erasure with a durable receipt.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Native large Library title and add/filter toolbar, date headings, a compact adaptive image grid, and three system tabs. Empty state names the next action. Immersive detail hides root tabs so original/download/delete actions remain unobstructed. Transfer rows preserve identity and explain progress or recovery.
