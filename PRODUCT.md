# PinHaoYun
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
ios

## Stack
SwiftUI, iOS 17+, native Apple frameworks; existing Next.js APIs and AWS backend.

## Users
Invited testers, then Australia and overseas Chinese-speaking users. Simplified Chinese and English.

## Product Purpose
Privately store, browse and retrieve original photos, videos and Live Photos. Existing Web and iOS accounts share the same cloud library in production.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Core delivery: email authentication, versioned policy acknowledgement, timeline, original uploads/downloads, durable transfers, quota, media deletion and account erasure within 30 days. The 0.2 personal-device beta adds automatic camera backup, Wi-Fi/cellular options and local daily time windows. Physical Photos/iCloud/background acceptance and three days of use remain pending. Maps and payments follow separately. Isolated Sydney AWS resources are mandatory for development.

## Brand Commitments
Keep PinHaoYun's name. Use native iPhone navigation and controls. The user approved building interactive SwiftUI screens directly, with Library, Transfers and Account tabs and browsing as the primary task.

## Product Principles
- Preserve originals and Live Photo pairs.
- Describe transfer and deletion state honestly.
- Keep credentials on the server or in Keychain.
- Account deletion affects both clients, never the device's Photos library.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, dark appearance, system motion preferences and 44-point touch targets.
